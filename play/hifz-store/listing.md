# Tibyan — Play listing (en-US)

**App name (30):** Tibyan - Quran hifz planner
**Short description (80):** Plan and track Qur'an memorisation: sabaq, sabqi and manzil, with your teacher.

**Full description:**

Tibyan tracks hifz the way it is taught: three daily portions, graded recitation and a revision cycle.

SABAQ, SABQI, MANZIL
Log today's new lesson, recent revision and older revision in a few taps.

KNOW WHICH PAGES ARE WEAK
Tibyan keeps a strength for every page from your graded recitations, so you can see which pages really need work, not just the ones that feel weak.

A REVISION PLAN THAT FITS
Your manzil cycle is scheduled for you, and Tibyan warns you before revision starts taking all of your time.

SEE WHERE YOU ARE HEADED
Progress, trends, a calendar of your sessions and a projection of when you will finish at your current pace.

LEARN WITH A TEACHER
Connect with your teacher, or your students. A teacher can follow a student's record and log what was heard in class.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
No ads, no tracking. Sign in with your email, no password. Export everything or delete your account from Settings at any time.
