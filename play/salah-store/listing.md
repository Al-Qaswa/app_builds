# Salah — Play listing (en-US)

**App name (30):** Salah - prayer times & qada
**Short description (80):** Prayer times for your city, an honest record of the five, and the qada you owe.

**Full description:**

Salah keeps your salah, quietly.

PRAYER TIMES
See the next prayer and how long until it, worked out from the sun for the city you choose. Pick the calculation method your region follows.

MARK EACH PRAYER
Tap a prayer to record it: on time, late, in congregation, missed, or not owed. Salah never marks a prayer as missed for a day you didn't open the app.

QADA
Keep count of the prayers you are making up, including the qada you owed before you installed the app, and watch the number come down.

YOUR RECORD
Look back over your days on a calendar. No streaks to break, no red for a missed prayer, nothing that shouts.

PRIVATE
No ads, no tracking.
